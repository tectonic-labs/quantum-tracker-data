2Z is the utility token of the DoubleZero Network, issued by the DoubleZero Foundation as a canonical SPL token on Solana. A token cannot be more quantum-safe than the chain it executes on, so 2Z's transaction-signing, consensus, and networking exposure are inherited from Solana — that inheritance is the ceiling on its post-quantum posture. On top of the host-chain inheritance, the Foundation directly controls the surfaces a token issuer is responsible for: an active SPL mint authority, a metadata/update authority, the Foundation Token Treasury custody keys, and a separate validator-operated DoubleZero ledger that drives 2Z token flows. Every disclosed and inferred signing surface across those components is the elliptic-curve scheme Ed25519, the Solana-native standard.

As of this report, the Foundation has published no post-quantum migration plan for any 2Z-controlled surface, and no plan to retire elliptic-curve cryptography from the mint authority, the Treasury custody multisigs, the metadata authority, or the DoubleZero ledger validator set. The token launched in mainnet-beta on 2 October 2025 and remains in beta. 2Z is a single-chain token with no published cross-chain deployment and is an unbacked utility token with no off-chain reserve, so two of the six categories do not apply to it.

## 1. Host Chain Aggregate

**Grade: F ❌**

2Z is canonically issued on **Solana** as the SPL token `2Z` (mint `J6pQQ3FAcJQeWPPGppWRb4nM8jU3wLyYbRrLh7feMfvd`), launched at the DoubleZero mainnet-beta token-generation event on 2 October 2025. The token's transaction signing, consensus, and networking exposure are the host chain's responsibility, not the token issuer's — and the token cannot be more quantum-safe than the chain it executes on. Host Chain Aggregate inherits the worst stoplight across the chains a token runs on.

DoubleZero's *network* layer is described as chain-agnostic for validator routing, supporting Solana, Sui, Aptos, Celestia, and Avalanche validators on the data plane, but the 2Z *asset* itself currently lives only on [Solana](../chains/l1/solana.md) ([Foundation statement](https://x.com/doublezero/status/1963034542203138140)). Because there is a single host chain, there is no `(m/n)` distribution qualifier — the inherited rating is simply Solana's worst category.

**Current state.** Solana's worst categories are quantum-exposed, which sets the inherited Host Chain Aggregate rating at the bottom of the scale. The 2Z SPL mint and mainnet-beta launch are documented on-chain and in the Foundation's announcement ([mint announcement](https://x.com/doublezero/status/1973015184601178378); [SPL mint on Solscan](https://solscan.io/token/J6pQQ3FAcJQeWPPGppWRb4nM8jU3wLyYbRrLh7feMfvd)).

## 2. Admin / Privileged Roles

**Grade: F ❌**

The DoubleZero Foundation is the issuer of 2Z and the operator of the Foundation Token Treasury. Unlike tokens that burn their mint and freeze authorities at launch, 2Z's mint authority must remain active because the Foundation's published economic model uses dynamic supply management — protocol inflation for rewards and security, fee burning, and contemplated "Treasury Sales" of 2Z to fund operations.

**Current state.** The privileged-role surfaces are:

- **Mint authority** — active and Foundation-controlled. The 10B genesis supply is not the cap on the SPL mint; the Foundation's tokenomics disclosure describes ongoing inflation and burning flows, and the SEC no-action submission contemplates programmatic Provider Payments and Computation Payments funded from the token ([Morrison Foerster analysis](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251020-sec-staff-issues-no-action-relief-for-depin-doublezero-foundation); [Decrypt analysis](https://decrypt.co/342186/what-sec-no-action-letter-means-solana-depin-token-doublezero)). The exact multisig threshold, signer roster, and HSM posture are not publicly disclosed.
- **Freeze authority** — status not publicly disclosed by the Foundation. The on-chain mint header is the canonical source for its current state ([SPL mint on Solscan](https://solscan.io/token/J6pQQ3FAcJQeWPPGppWRb4nM8jU3wLyYbRrLh7feMfvd)).
- **Metadata / update authority** — Foundation-controlled, governing SPL Token Metadata Program updates (name, symbol, URI).
- **Foundation Token Treasury** — holds the unlocked ecosystem allocation plus vesting allocations as they unlock; custody is presumed to be Solana-native multisigs, with signer rosters and thresholds not publicly disclosed ([tokenomics summary](https://phemex.com/academy/what-is-doublezero-2z); [allocation breakdown](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/doublezero-explained)).

All disclosed and inferred signing surfaces use the elliptic-curve scheme Ed25519, the Solana-native standard. 2Z was designed as a non-governance utility token at launch — holders have no dividend, profit-sharing, or voting rights — so the only privileged surface to rate is the Foundation's own keys.

**Planned future work.** The launch is recent (2 October 2025), and nothing in the Foundation's published materials — the tokenomics disclosure, SEC no-action submission, whitepapers, or public posts — describes migrating any of these keys to post-quantum primitives. No concrete post-quantum proposal currently exists for the admin surfaces.

## 5. Other Token-Specific Crypto

**Grade: F ❌**

DoubleZero ships a token-specific cryptographic surface that most SPL utility tokens lack: a **custom ledger** that the Foundation describes as the system of record for managing the network and its token flows outside of Solana ([Foundation statement](https://x.com/doublezero/status/1963034542203138140)). The SEC no-action submission elaborates that Resource Providers receive Computation Payments specifically for maintaining the DoubleZero ledger ([Morrison Foerster analysis](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251020-sec-staff-issues-no-action-relief-for-depin-doublezero-foundation)) — making it a distinct, validator-operated ledger whose state ultimately drives 2Z token flows.

**Current state.** The DoubleZero ledger inherits its cryptographic stack from a Solana-style validator architecture:

- **Validator signing** uses the elliptic-curve scheme Ed25519, consistent with the Solana-derived stack and the Foundation's published validator setup guidance ([validator setup guide](https://www.cherryservers.com/blog/set-up-doublezero-on-a-solana-validator)).
- **Edge filtering** uses a two-ring architecture with custom FPGA hardware at network ingress for spam filtering, packet and signature deduplication, and signature verification of incoming validator traffic; the scheme verified at the FPGA edge is Ed25519 ([Figment overview](https://www.figment.io/insights/doublezero-first-look-the-internet-layer-for-solana-and-beyond/)).
- **Proof relays** are cited in third-party explainers as early connectors for cross-EVM proof relaying; the protocols referenced rely on elliptic-curve primitives.

The 2Z token's economic accounting is gated on the integrity of this custom ledger — its validator set ultimately drives Provider Payments, fee burns, and inflation rewards that settle as SPL transfers on Solana.

**Planned future work.** No post-quantum migration of the custom-ledger validator signing or the FPGA verification path is on any published DoubleZero roadmap; no concrete post-quantum proposal currently exists for this surface.

## Issuer & Governance

DoubleZero is operated by the **DoubleZero Foundation**, a memberless Cayman Islands foundation company established to support the development, decentralization, security, and adoption of the DoubleZero Network ([Morrison Foerster analysis](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251020-sec-staff-issues-no-action-relief-for-depin-doublezero-foundation)).

2Z is explicitly not a governance token at launch: holders have no dividend, profit-sharing, or voting rights, and there is no on-chain DAO vote surface. Per the SEC no-action submission, network operations split across three participant classes — Network Providers, who contribute fiber capacity; Resource Providers, who maintain the DoubleZero ledger and compute Provider Payment amounts; and Users, who pay 2Z for low-latency routing services. The Foundation directly controls protocol parameters, the Token Treasury, and programmatic transfers. Decentralization of governance is positioned as a forward-looking goal but is not active at launch.

Product and regulatory commitments are disclosed through the Foundation's site and journal ([DoubleZero site](https://doublezero.xyz/); [Foundation no-action announcement](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/sec-grants-doublezero-no-action-letter-for-2z-token-model)), the published 2Z tokenomics disclosure and whitepapers ([tokenomics disclosure](https://doublezero.xyz/2z-tokenomics-disclosure.pdf); [whitepaper](https://doublezero.xyz/whitepaper.pdf)), and the SEC no-action submission ([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/files/corpfin/no-action/doublezero-final-conformed-092625.pdf)). Any future post-quantum commitment for the token would be expected to surface in those venues. The intel file records no dated, on-record PQC milestone published by the Foundation.
