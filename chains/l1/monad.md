Monad is an EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain launched in November 2025, designed for high throughput via parallel execution and speculative transaction processing. It is Ethereum-compatible at the transaction, signing, and contract levels, and all cryptography on the chain is standard ECDSA secp256k1 and the Ethereum EVM precompile set.
