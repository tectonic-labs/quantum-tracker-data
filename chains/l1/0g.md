## Summary

0G is a modular AI-focused Layer 1 blockchain that launched its Aristotle mainnet in September 2025. It combines a Cosmos SDK / CometBFT consensus layer with an EVM-compatible execution layer (migrated from Geth to Reth in early 2026) and a dedicated data availability layer. As of mid-2026, no post-quantum cryptography work has been published or announced across any of 0G's three layers.

The chain-specific concern beyond the standard Cosmos/EVM stack is the 0G Data Availability layer, which relies on KZG polynomial commitments over BLS12-381 pairings, BLS aggregate signatures from DA nodes, and an EC-based verifiable random function for node sampling. These are all quantum-vulnerable constructions. No STARK-based or PQC alternative to any of these has appeared in 0G's published roadmap.

## Other Features

**Grade: F ❌**

The 0G Data Availability layer is the chain's signature technical contribution, and it carries substantial quantum exposure. Three cryptographic constructions underpin DA:

**Current state**: The DA layer uses [KZG polynomial commitments](https://0g.ai/blog/0g-s-data-availability-layer) (based on BLS12-381 pairings) to encode blob data, BLS aggregate signatures from DA nodes attesting to blob availability, and an EC-based VRF for unpredictably selecting storage and DA nodes to perform sampling. All three depend on the hardness of the elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem, which a sufficiently powerful quantum computer running Shor's algorithm would break.

**Planned future work**: No STARK-based or PQC alternative for any of these DA-layer primitives has been identified in [0G's published documentation](https://docs.0g.ai/) or roadmap as of early May 2026. The 0G DA roadmap as described in public materials focuses on throughput, cost, and decentralization improvements, not cryptographic migration.

## Governance

The EVM execution client (the Geth-to-Reth migration in early 2026) was an operator-level migration that did not require an on-chain governance vote.
